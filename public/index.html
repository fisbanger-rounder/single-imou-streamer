      Setup checklist

      1. Create an app at open.imoulife.com (or open.imou.com for China) to get App ID / App Secret.
      2. Bind your camera serial + verification code under My Resources.
      3. Put credentials in .env next to server.js (copy .env.example) and pick your region’s base URL.
      4. Restart the server, enter the device SN here and press Connect.

      Two-way talk needs microphone permission and a secure origin (localhost or HTTPS). PTZ needs a pan/tilt model such as Ranger series.
